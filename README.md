## 💡 Key Features That Solve Your Daily Hassles

### 1. Instant Alerts for Rogue Tabs (RAM & Internet Hogs)
You shouldn't have to guess why your computer is suddenly running slow. tabDelta constantly watches your open tabs in the background.
* **Custom Thresholds:** You set the performance limits that match your computer.
* **Instant Notifications:** The exact moment a background tab goes rogue—like a heavy social media feed or a hidden video streaming in the background—tabDelta sends you a direct desktop notification.
* **Speed Up Your PC:** Instantly know exactly which specific tab to consider closing to free up your memory and recover your computer's speed.

### 2. Smart Attention Tracking (Where Did the Day Go?)
Traditional tools just track your passive history links, which doesn't tell the true story. tabDelta intelligently maps out your actual human focus.
* **Active Engagement:** It measures real interaction (like active scrolling, typing, or moving your mouse).
* **Automatic Background Pausing:** The exact second a tab is hidden or pushed into the background, its attention tracking drops to zero. 
* **Focus Badges:** Easily view your time cleanly split into **Deep Work**, **Focus**, or **Idle** so you can see where your energy is actually going.

### 3. GlassWire-Style Internet Data Counters
Ever wonder why your internet data is running out so fast? tabDelta features a visual internet monitoring panel.
* See exactly how many Megabytes (MB) each tab downloads and uploads during your browsing session.
* Automatically organizes website traffic into helpful buckets like Streaming Video, Images, or Hidden Tracking Scripts so you can spot bandwidth hogs.


## 🔒 Our Absolute Privacy Commitment

tabDelta was built for personal insight, not surveillance. Because your data belongs to you, our architecture follows a strict **Local-First** design:

* **Zero Cloud Servers:** We maintain no remote servers, require no user accounts, and have no login screens. 
* **No Data Selling:** Because your data never leaves your physical machine, it is physically impossible for us to track, share, or sell your browsing history to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
* **Safe Cleanups:** The extension features an automatic 90-day cleanup loop to keep your local drive light, alongside a one-click "Clear Data" button to hard-wipe everything instantly.
